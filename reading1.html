Reading Response 1

This reading made me see restaurant websites as more than just practical places to find menus or hours. The Astro Restaurant website stood out because its inconsistent fonts and outdated design gave it personality. What might normally be considered “bad” design actually made the restaurant feel human. The article also made me reflect on how modern platforms make websites easier to build but often cause them to feel similar. Convenience can come at the cost of creative control.

Jasmine’s point that nostalgia is a privilege also made me think. Handmade websites can create extra work for restaurant employees, so returning to older tools is not the best solution. Instead, restaurant websites should offer more useful functions that attract people to use them. Features like ordering, reservations, and current specials could make the website worth visiting while still expressing the restaurant’s personality.
