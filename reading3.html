Reading Response 3

This is a reading response to “My website is a shifting house next to a river of knowledge. What could yours be?” by Laurel Schwulst in 2018.

What stood out to me most was the idea that a website does not have to be finished or perfect to be meaningful. I usually think of a personal website as a portfolio where someone shows their best work. Schwulst’s comparison of a website to a garden helped me imagine it as a place where ideas can grow over time. As a student, I sometimes focus so much on making an assignment feel complete that I leave out the small experiments or unfinished thoughts that helped me get there. This reading made me wonder what it would look like to give those parts of my work their own space.

As I learn more about design, I want to think about my website as a space that can change with me. I would want it to include things I am curious about, ideas from class, and projects I am still figuring out. I also liked the idea of making choices beyond the layouts that social media gives us, even though having that much freedom feels a little overwhelming. The reading helped me see that I could start small. I do not need to know exactly what my website will become before I begin building it.
