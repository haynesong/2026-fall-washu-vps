Reading Response 2

This is a reading Response to the article The New Turing Test by Elan Ullendorf

What stood out to me most in “The New Turing Test” was the idea that instead of only asking whether something was created by AI, we should ask how much humanity it contains. I sometimes feel like my own writing sounds like AI because I lose my voice and focus only on getting the facts down or pushing an assignment out. Although I do not have much experience with design yet, the reading helped me realize that design can have the same problem. Something can be polished and meet every requirement but still feel generic, as if anyone could have made it.

As I learn more about design, I want to create work that feels like it came from someone, and I want that someone to be recognizably me. I want my choices to reflect my personality, perspective, and intentions instead of only showing that I completed the assignment correctly. The reading reminded me that strong design is not just about how something looks. It is also about creating a real connection between the person who made it and the person experiencing it.
